Privacy Policy

One Wish Willow uses customer information only for ordinary shop purposes: taking orders, reviewing payment, answering questions, and getting the package to the right place without unnecessary mystery.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, order quantity, payment review details, and basic website information needed to operate the store.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to process orders, confirm payment instructions, send order updates, respond to support messages, prevent misuse, and improve the checkout experience.

Retention

Order records may be kept for fulfillment, support, accounting, and dispute-resolution needs. We keep them only as long as reasonably useful for those purposes.

Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Limited information may be shared with service providers that help with email, hosting, shipping, payment review, fraud prevention, or customer support.
